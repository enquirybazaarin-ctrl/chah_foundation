Our Story

From one classroom in 2015 to twelve districts today.

Chah Foundation began when a group of teachers pooled money to keep six girls in school. Ten years later, we run programmes across girls' education, adolescent health, skill training, and child marriage prevention — reaching over 20,000 girls every year.

Mission & Vision

Why we exist

Mission

To give every girl — regardless of birth, caste, or circumstance — the education, health, and safety she needs to write her own future.

Vision

An India where no girl is pulled out of school, married before she turns 18, or denied a livelihood because of where she was born.

Milestones

Ten years, one step at a time

2015
Six girls sponsored into school by founding teachers in Jaipur.
2017
Registered as a Public Charitable Trust. First health camp held.
2019
80G and 12A certifications received. Programme extended to 4 districts.
2021
Skill training centres launched. 500 girls trained in tailoring and computers.
2023
Child marriage prevention committees set up in 120 villages.
2026
Over 12,000 girls supported. 340 villages reached across 12 districts.
